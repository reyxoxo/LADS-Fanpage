Of course, this game wouldn't be what it is without all the elements coming together to make this game!!

Destiny Café

This is where you first come into the game, kind of like the homescreen.

Here, you can choose what you want to do!

There's always a love interest waiting for you at Destiny Café, so don't feel shy to interact with him!

Gacha Pull System

Love and Deepspace incorporates a gacha pull system for in-game events,

similar to Genshin Impact or Cookie Run Kingdom.

Wishpools

When you make a pull from the wish pull, you can get either a 3-star, 4-star, or 5-star card.

Here is one primary wish pool in the game, XSpace Echo. Limited wishpulls appear during events

for players to get Limited Event cards- mostly 5-stars

Galaxy Explorer Rewards

Similar to wish pulls, but using Magnets rather than diamonds.

You can obtain standard 3-star, 4-star, or 5-star cards.

Empyrean Wishes

Wish cards you use to pull for items in standard wish pools.

Deepspace Wishes (Can be limited or not)

Wish cards you use to pull for items in limited or rerun wish pools.

Solo Banners(Can be limited or not)

During this event, players aim to get one limited card of a certain love interest,

depending on what event it is. Examples include Birthday events, or more recently Passionate Appraisal (Sylus)

Multibanners

During multibanners, you can pull for limited cards of all the love interests.

Examples include Witnessed By Deepspace, Spring and Flowers, and You and Midsummer.

Myth Cards

During Myth events, you can pull for two cards of a specific LI to unlock a

battle companion you can fight alongside. Examples include Lemurian Sea God (Rafayel)

God of Annihilation (Zayne) and King of Darknight (Xavier).

Reruns

This is when a limited card comes back for a temporary amount of time.

Myths are the most common events to rerun, occurring yearly.

Battle

In certain stages of the main story, or even to complete daily quests,

you can battle Wanderers for rewards and diamonds.

Stamina

A requirement needed to complete Bounty Hunts or Core Hunts.

you can battle Wanderers for rewards and diamonds.

Stellactrum
Bounty Hunts

In Bounty Hunt, you can fight Wanderers for certain rewards.

For example, Lemonette drops yellow and purple ascension crystals,

which can be used to ascend your memories with purple and yellow stellactrums.

Core Hunt

In Core Hunt, you battle Wanderers for rewards like protocores, which can be used to enhance memories.

UNICORNS Operations

Explore areas of Tanakahli for rewards and information on the areas visited.

Abyssal Chaos

Complete deductions to earn rewards in the form of diamonds and artifacts.

Deepspace Trials

Similar to bounty hunt, except you get diamonds after completion.

Training room

You can complete training sessions with weapons or companions in the training room

to learn how to fight better- or you can do it just for diamonds.

Memories

Memories are cards you obtain from wish pulls or galaxy explorer rewards.

They can be leveled up, ranked up, and ascended to make them stronger in battle.

Affinity

The more time you spend with a certain love interest, the more their affinity increases.

This can be beneficial in battle, as it provides a small bonus to your team.

By Your Side

Here, you can read short stories about the Love Interests.

Claw Machine

As the name suggests, you can get plushes with the claw machine.

Be on the lookout for plushies that are exclusively for your love interest. Here are a few examples.

Xavier - Galaxy Kid

Zayne - Happy Snowman

Rafayel - Artsy Birb

Sylus - Grumpy Crow

Caleb - Sunny Apple

Kitty Cards

A fun two-player game to pass time.

By winning games and earning plushies and Meow Badges, you can earn chocolate and diamonds.

Secret Times/Tender Moments

Mini audio dramas you can listen to, for both diamonds and added interaction for the love interests.

These are based on four star cards you obtain through the wish pool(s) or through the Galaxy Explorer rewards.

Glint Photobooth

To take photos!! You can use different backgrounds, poses, and outfits to make cute pictures.

There's also an AR mode, to take apicture with your irl self too!

Journal

Quite literally a journal, to put in pictures you take during the game.

World Underneath

Here, you can read short stories about side characters and other in-game storylines, like Mischief (Luke and Kieran) or No Morning (Greyson)

Promise and Promise Cards

Promises are Cumulative rewards that increase as you do more daily or weekly tasks- or interact with the love interests more.

Promise cards are paid 4-star cards that you can earn by completing promise and secret promise levels.

Daily/Weekly Tasks

Do these to get rewards in the form of Diamonds, XP, and stamina cans.

Protocores

Items you can use to enhance memories, making them stronger in battle.

Moments Posts

MC's phone, where you can message all the guys and see their posts on social media!

Diamonds

The primary currency to get wishes from the shop.

Can be used for photobooth poses and other items as well.

Crystals

An exclusive currency available for paid players

Gold

Primarily used to ascend memories, as a certain amount of gold is required to ascend a memory.

Chocolate

Can be obtained from playing kitty cards or going to the claw machine for plushies.

Heartsand

When your memory is at rank, extra memory shards are converted to heartsand,

which you can use to get ascension bottles or Empyrean Wishes.
