Destiny Café

This is where you first come into the game, kind of like the homescreen. Here, you can choose what you want to do! There's always a love interest waiting for you at destiny café, so don't feel shy to interact with him!

Gacha Pull System

Love and Deepspace incorporates a gacha pull system for in-game events, similar to Genshin Impact or Cookie Run Kingdom.

Wish Pool(s)

When you make a pull from the wish pull, you can get either a 3-star, 4-star, or 5-star card. There is one primary wish pool in the game, XSpace Echo. Limited wishpulls appear during events for players to get Limited Event cards- mostly 5-stars.

Galaxy Explorer Rewards

Similar to wish pulls, but using Magnets rather than diamonds. You can obtain standard 3-star, 4-star, or 5-star cards.

Empyrean Wishes

Wish cards you use to pull for items in standard wish pools.

Deepspace Wishes (Can be limited or not)

Wish cards you use to pull for items in limited or rerun wish pools.

Solo Banners

During this event, players aim to get one limited card of a certain love interest, depending on what event it is. Examples include Birthday events, or more recently Passionate Appraisal (Sylus),

Multibanners

During multibanners, you can pull for limited cards of all the love interests, contrary to solo banners. Examples include Witnessed By Deepspace, Spring and Flowers, and You and Midsummer.

Myth Cards

During Myth events, you can pull for two cards of a specific LI to unlock a battle companion you can fight alongside. Examples include Lemurian Sea God (Rafayel) God of Annihilation (Zayne) and Abysm Sovereign (Sylus).

Reruns

This is when a limited card comes back for a temporary amount of time. Myths are the most common events to rerun, occurring yearly.

Battle In certain stages of the main story, or even to complete daily quests, you can battle Wanderers for rewards and diamonds.

Stamina A requirement needed to complete Bounty Hunts or Core Hunts. Stellactrum Bounty Hunts In Bounty Hunt, you can fight Wanderers for certain rewards. For example, Lemonette drops yellow and purple ascension crystals, which can be used to ascend your memories with purple and yellow stellactrums. . Core Hunt In Core Hunt, you battle Wanderers for rewards like protocores, which can be used to enhance memories. The Protocore guide is located here.

UNICORNS Operations Explore areas of Tanakahli for rewards and information on the areas visited.

Abyssal Chaos

Complete deductions to earn rewards in the form of diamonds and artifacts.

Deepspace Trials

Similar to bounty hunt, except you get diamonds after completion.

Training room

You can complete training sessions with weapons or companions in the training room to learn how to fight better- or you can do it just for diamonds.

Companion Rehearsal

Similar to regular training with weapons in the training room, but you learn how to use a companion's active, passive, and resonance skill. You can also do this for diamonds.

Memories Upgrading memories

You can use ascension bottles to upgrade your memories, eventually ascending them at the max level of 80.

Rank Up

Using memory shards in the wish pool, you can rank up your memories a maximum of three times.

Ascending memories

When you get a memory to level 80, you can ascend memories with an ascension heart, to unlock not only a nice visual effect but also more strength in battle.

Affinity

The more time you spend with a certain love interest, the more their affinity increases. This can be beneficial in battle, as it provides a small bonus to your team.

Dating

You can spend time with a certain love interest to raise affinity and to take a break from the battles.

A fun two-player game to pass time. By winning games and earning plushies and Meow Badges, you can earn chocolate and diamonds.

Secret Times

Mini audio dramas you can listen to, for both diamonds and added interaction for the love interests. These are based on four star cards you obtain through the wish pool(s) or through the Galaxy Explorer rewards..
