Alongside the Love Interests, there are other characters you can interact with!

These people may be with or against you.

(I in fact have not completed all of main story yet :/ there may be a few important people missing in this list).

Tara

A colleague of Xavier and MC in the Hunter's Association.

Captain Jenna

The supervisor/captain of rookie team UNICORNS- the part of the Hunters Association that Xavier, MC, and Tara are in.

Nero

A colleague of MC, Tara, and Xavier. He is a data analyst at the Hunters Association, and is relatively antisocial.

Andrew

The Captain of the Data Analysis Branch, and the supervisor of Tara and Nero.

Greyson

A co-worker of Zayne's, a cardiac surgeon at Akso Hospital.

Thomas

A close friend of Rafayel's, as well as his agent to organize his art exhibitions and events.

Josephine

A former worker at the Gaia research center, took in MC and Caleb as children.

Jeremiah

A florist at the flower shop Philo.

Luke and Kieran

These twins are two of Sylus's henchmen in Onychinus. They have the ability to sense and feel each other's pain, as well as communicate mentally. Their faces have not been revealed.

Lumiere

A mysterious hunter who killed numurous Wanderers during the Chronorift Catastrope- and saved the lives of many people, as well as MC. He is Xavier's Alter ego.

Wanderers(Entity)

Wanderers attack humans witht he intent to harm. They are typically not self-aware, and lack intelligence.

EVER (Organization)

A scientific organization that has been criticized for their inhuman ways of experimentation.

Onichynus (Organization)

An illegal protocore trade organization, headed by Sylus.
