This is a fanpage created for the Chinese otome game, Love and Deepspace, where you can learn about all the characters and elements of this game! Think of it as a beginner's guide. I haven't been playing for that long, so information may be incorrect

You are the main character in this game. Universally known as MC, you navigate your life in futuristic Linkon City, meeting people and forming connections that may have hidden and deeper motives in them.

This is a game I really enjoy, and I just had to make a fanpage of what is currently one of my biggest hyperfixations at the moment. Click on the links above to navigate the website and learn more!
